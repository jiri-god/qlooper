## Preparation

Leading near-silence trimmed with 0.5 ms preattack retained; peak level adjusted to
-6 dBFS (-12 dBFS for soft kick); final 5 ms faded to zero. Retained source sample
rates and channels, encoded to lossless 24-bit FLAC. No pitch/time modification.
These are starter choices for audition, not a claim of perceptual loudness matching.
Original full sample libraries are not required to run Sample Pad.
